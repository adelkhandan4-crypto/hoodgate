# HoodGate artwork

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool. The original generated PNGs are retained locally under the image-generation output directory. The production assets use WebP encoding without composition changes.

## Final assets

- `public/art/gate.webp`: sculptural gateway, hero and connection illustrations.
- `public/art/agent.webp`: computational core, agent and identity illustrations.
- `public/art/modules.webp`: modular service layers, marketplace and developer illustrations.

## Generation prompts

### gate

Premium editorial 3D product illustration for HoodGate, a machine-commerce website. A sculptural square gateway made of four thick brushed champagne gold rectangular bars, suspended at a 3/4 angle, with delicate golden filament paths flowing through its central empty opening. Monumental but compact, exquisite chamfered edges, tiny engineered engraved line details, realistic metal reflections. Single centered object isolated on near-black #080909 background, ample black margin, no floor, subtle warm halo. Palette exclusively pale gold, dark gold and black. 1:1 composition, dramatic sophisticated studio lighting, tactile physically based materials. No typography, no logos, no watermark, no coins, no robot faces. Actual art asset, not a website mockup.

### agent

Premium 3D editorial art for a black-and-gold machine-commerce interface. One luminous computational core: small faceted golden sphere suspended inside three delicate orthogonal dark champagne-gold orbital rings, a sparse constellation of tiny points connecting to the core via fine golden wires. Precise engineered object, beautiful translucent amber glass and brushed gold, ray-traced studio lighting, isolated on near-black #080909 background with generous negative space and no floor. Square composition. Elegant restrained luxury, avoid sci-fi neon. No words, logos, watermark, crypto coin symbols or humanoid robots. Actual illustration asset.

### modules

Luxury editorial 3D illustration for a machine-commerce service catalog, black and gold. An exploded assembly of five precision-engineered rounded-square modular plates, stacked vertically in isometric view with space between each plate. Champagne gold edges, black obsidian surfaces, fine glowing gold traces and a small square central socket on the upper plate. Sparse dust particles and thin connection lines between layers. Centered sculptural composition on near-black #080909 background, generous margin, no floor, sophisticated realistic studio reflections. Square image, exquisite tactile detail. No text, letters, numbers, logos, watermark or UI screenshot.
